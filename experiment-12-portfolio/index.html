01 · THE CHALLENGE

Making scholarship discovery easier

Students may need to search across multiple sources, interpret eligibility requirements and identify the documents needed to apply. SCHOLARO brings these tasks into one guided experience.

PROBLEM 01

Scattered information

Scholarship information can be distributed across different portals and announcements.

PROBLEM 02

Eligibility confusion

Students need to understand course, income, academic and category requirements.

PROBLEM 03

Application preparation

Document requirements and application steps can be difficult to track.

02 · DESIGN PROCESS

From user needs to interface decisions

The lab work explores user research, personas, affinity mapping, wireframes, mockups, micro-interactions and prototyping. Any simulated research material must be identified as hypothetical rather than real participant data.

RESEARCH

Understand the problem

Organize student needs around discovery, eligibility, documents and application management.

STRUCTURE

Plan the experience

Use wireframes to establish page hierarchy, navigation and content placement.

VALIDATE

Demonstrate interactions

Use component states and a clickable flow to show how the interface behaves.

03 · DESIGN EXPLORATIONS

Core SCHOLARO screens

Three representative screens form the central discovery journey.

01 · Scholarship discovery

Introduces the service and highlights recommended opportunities.

02 · Search and filters

Helps students narrow opportunities using relevant criteria.

03 · Scholarship details

Groups overview, eligibility and document information.

04 · INTERACTION & PROTOTYPE

From static screens to a usable flow

The companion prototype demonstrates navigation from discovery to scholarship details, switching information tabs and saving or unsaving an opportunity.
